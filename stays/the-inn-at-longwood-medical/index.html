Hotels in the Boston tourism district may add a 1.5% assessment (16.45% total, e.g. YOTEL help center, Meco T&C). Google Hotels list prices are "nightly price with fees" i.e. mandatory fees included, taxes NOT included unless an offer says "taxes + fees".
